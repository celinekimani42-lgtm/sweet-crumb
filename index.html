Baker Policies & Delivery Terms

1. 24-Hour Notice Guarantee

Orders confirmed with a 50% deposit are guaranteed to be baked the same morning and delivered within exactly 24 hours across Narok Town and its environs.

2. 50% M-Pesa Commitment Deposit

Because all birthday cakes are customized and perishable, baking begins immediately after receipt of the 50% commitment deposit via Buy Goods Till 9842105. Remaining balance is cleared upon doorstep delivery.

3. Delivery Zones & Rider Rates

Complimentary delivery within Narok Town CBD and Total Area. Maasai Mara University, Limanet, and outlying county locations incur a flat rider fee of KSh 150–300.

4. Changes & Inscription Updates

Plaque text or delivery time changes are accommodated at zero fee up to 12 hours prior to scheduled dispatch time.
