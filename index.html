Baker Guidelines

Terms & Baker Policies

Ensuring smooth celebrations across Narok County.

1. 24-Hour Notice Guarantee

Standard signature cakes require 24 hours advance notice. Same-day emergency orders may be accommodated based on sponge availability with an express fee.

2. 50% M-Pesa Commitment Deposit

Because all birthday cakes are customized and perishable, baking begins only after receipt of a 50% commitment deposit via Buy Goods Till 9842105. Balance is cleared upon doorstep delivery.

3. Delivery Radius & Fees

Free delivery within Narok Town CBD and Total Area. Maasai Mara University, Limanet, and outlying areas incur a small rider fee of KSh 150–KSh 300.

4. Changes & Cancellations

Changes to piping inscription or delivery time are accepted up to 12 hours before scheduled dispatch time.
